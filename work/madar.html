CASE STUDY · ENTERPRISE HR · FLUTTER REWRITE

Rewriting an HR app that could no longer be maintained.

Madar is an enterprise HR self-service app. The legacy codebase had become unmaintainable, so I rebuilt it from scratch in Flutter as primary developer, on a modular Clean Architecture.

The problem

The legacy HR codebase was no longer maintainable. Patching it would have kept the problems, so the decision was to rebuild from scratch.

What I did

  • Led the rewrite as primary developer, with about 85% of 400+ commits.
  • Designed and built 17 modules: attendance, leaves, overtime, business missions, transfers, training, certificates and more. That is 100+ screens, 157 API endpoints and about 80K lines of code.
  • Arabic and English with RTL from the start, biometric login and root detection.

Architecture

libScreens and one Cubit per screen
↓ depends on
diget_it registration that wires the layers together
↓ depends on
dataRetrofit clients, models and repository implementations
↓ depends on
domainEntities and repository contracts
↓ depends on
commonShared types and utilities used by every layer

Outcome

A maintainable replacement for the legacy app: 17 modules on separate common, domain, data and di packages, in Arabic and English.
